About me

I'm a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) (Expected Aug 2026), currently based in Harrison, NJ. I specialize in Program Analysis and Formal Verification, where I develop tools to detect sources of nondeterminism and inconsistency in unsupervised learning using SMT solvers, and methods to control Neural Network generalization via MILP. My research has been published in ICSE and ASE, and I bring additional professional software engineering experience from my time at Samsung R&D Institute.
